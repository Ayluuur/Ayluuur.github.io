About Me

Short Bio

I am now an M.S. student at the School of Computer Science, Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications, supervised by Xuehuai Shi.

My research interests include virtual reality, human-computer interaction, and machine learning, with a particular interest in generating realistic human motion from sparse HMD tracking signals.

I have published a CCF-B academic paper in IEEE ISMAR 2025, and hold 2 granted patents.
